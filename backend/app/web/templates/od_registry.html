Organizational Decision Registry

These six decisions are the project's blocking Open Decisions (docs/baseline/README.md). This platform never invents or auto-approves a value for any of them -- every row below starts Open and only moves to Proposed, then Approved/Rejected, through an explicit, rationale-bearing action by a Platform Owner. Dependent functionality (Layer 1 Rule 3, the entire Layer 3 risk engine) stays unconfigured and fails closed until the corresponding decision is both Approved and Activated.
